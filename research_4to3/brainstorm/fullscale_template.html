Haar scalograms of g85 and g98 at full resolution A window of 4000 positions and 2000 scales, one pixel per coefficient, sliding along the whole word

V. KeränenWorking notes, 3 November 2026A companion to Waves, wavelets and shadows

The scalogram on the main page shows a window of 1200 positions and 150 scales. This page shows 4000 positions and 2000 scales, 8 million coefficients, without any averaging. As there, the coefficient at position i and scale p compares the two halves of a window of length 2p:

Hp(i) = Ψ(u) − Ψ(v),   u = w[i .. i+p−1],   v = w[i+p .. i+2p−1].

A zero is an abelian square uv; ‖H‖1 = 2 is a one-letter near miss. The word is a2f exactly when there is no zero.

The picture is a window of positions i = i0, …, i0 + 3999 and scales p = 1, …, 2000. Move it with “picture from i”; it also moves by itself when you inspect a coefficient outside it. Cells with i + 2p − 1 beyond the end of the word are drawn flat. The counts and the list below cover the whole word, every i and every p up to half its length, whatever the picture shows. For example, the one-letter near miss with the largest scale in g85²(a) is at i = 4132, p = 1153, and in g98²(a) at i = 3875, p = 2017, above the top of the picture.

The choice “g85³(a), first part” uses the first 7999 letters of g85³(a), which begins with g85²(a). Its counts and list cover those 7999 letters.

Why the stripes lean, and the centred view. With i on the horizontal axis the colours run in stripes leaning backward at 45°. Write P(t) for the Parikh vector of the prefix of length t and c = i + p for the cut between u and v. Then

Hp(i) = 2P(c − 1) − P(c − p − 1) − P(c + p − 1).

The average drift of the walk cancels, and what is left are its fluctuations at three points. The centre has twice the weight of each end, so the colour depends mostly on how far the walk strays at the cut. A line of constant c is i = c − p: in the i-picture it leans backward at exactly 45°, because a pixel is one unit of i and one of p. Choose “c = i + p, centre” to put c on the horizontal axis: the stripes then stand vertical, and the two end terms show up as a fainter criss-cross at ±45°. Measured over about 50 000 coefficients with 50 ≤ p < 1500, the colour value ‖H‖1/√(2p) changes on average by 0.031 per step of p when c is held fixed, against 0.059 when the left end i or the right end i + 2p is held fixed (g85²(a); 0.030 against 0.058 for g98²(a)). In the centred view the empty corners lie on both sides: on the left u would start before the word, on the right v would end after it.
