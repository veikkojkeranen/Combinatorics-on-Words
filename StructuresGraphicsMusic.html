Avoidable regularities in strings

Structures, Programs, Graphics and Music of A-2-Free Strings

Abelian square-free words generated by the endomorphisms g85 and g98 and the substitution σ109 over four letters, and almost abelian square-free words over three letters

Veikko Keränen1996–2026Updated 12 November 2026

From this page you can find structural and graphical data of infinite abelian square-free strings generated by the endomorphisms g85 and g98 and the substitution σ109 over the four-letter alphabet {a, b, c, d}. These endomorphisms and substitutions, and 200 other cases, were found by Veikko Keränen with the help of many RAMK and Lapland UAS students. Veikko produced the major part of the related computer graphics.

Some of the links also concern the ternary alphabet {a, b, c}, where squares of period 1 are allowed but all other abelian squares are avoided.

In brief

g85
an abelian square-free endomorphism
g98
not abelian square-free as such, yet it produces an infinite abelian square-free D0L language
g85 + g98
together they produce an infinite abelian square-free DT0L language
σ109
(called g109 on the sub-pages) a powerful abelian square-free substitution: each letter has twelve image words of length 109, and every choice maps abelian square-free words to abelian square-free words. So the number of abelian square-free words over four letters grows exponentially (Theoret. Comput. Sci. 410 (2009) 3893–3900)
aa2f
almost abelian square-free: over {a, b, c}, only abelian squares of period 1 (aa, bb, cc) are allowed

The substitution σ109

Each letter has twelve image words of length 109, and any choice of images keeps abelian square-free words abelian square-free. On the sub-pages σ109 is usually called g109, and g109(a) is the first of its twelve image words of a.
