Near misses inside g852(a) – emergence of uxv and uv patterns
 ⁕ In uxv pattern, u and v are commutatively equivalent and x is a letter in {a,b,c,d}; in uv pattern, u and v differ only by one letter.

The following quite surprising situation holds for the four-letter abelian square-free endomorphism g85. The figures below show the occurrences of one uv and one uxv factor in g852(a).
