Developing a general search engine (April 2026)

A little bit of background for the next mission.

There are many long aa2f words over 3 letter alphabet at

https://algebra.fi/keranen/research/web110117/aa2f15796_aa2f25379_aa2f1928.html

They have been produced by very long computations, and we still do not know any systematic way to produce such, or infinitely long aa2f words over three letters.

At the page mentioned above, there are not only the original long words themselves but also their six permutations, together with the mirror words.

The permutations and mirror images are included because they allow one to quickly find certain kinds of factors (subwords).

The next goal is to study the factors of those long words and, at the same time, find new words that would fit as image words of some morphism h that could be used to generate an infinite aa2f structure, if such a structure exists. For example, a morphism h could be a mapping from well-known a2f words of over a 4-letter, or 8-letter, alphabet to words over a 3-letter alphabet (e.g. h: ALph_8* -> ALph_3*).

---
However, the main mission here is the following:

We should design and implement a search engine where the user can enter multiple search criteria at once, and all the matching parts of the long word (perhaps of on entire page) would be highlighted, with a different colour for each search criterion.

Possibly, perhaps at a later stage, the search criteria could also be regular expressions.

In any case, the search criterion need not be a set of words; for example, it could be defined by searching for words that have a certain Parikh vector (or belong to a given Parikh vector set).

In fact, there should be a possibility for the user to define any desired function as the search criterion.

We could at least initially target the search to websites. Of course, one would probably have to download the pages to one's own computer first, because the computation for some search criteria can be very heavy indeed.

How could such a generalised search engine be designed and implemented in practice?

Could it function for any webpage on the Internet?  Use of Java Script would be preferable.

Please advise. Thank you.
