You can also use the handy bookmarklet.js tool by first adding it as a bookmark.



**Installing Word Search bookmarklet.js**



Create a new bookmark site (right-click in the bookmarks bar → "Add page" / "New Bookmark"), and paste this as its address:



javascript:(function(){var d=document,s=d.createElement('script');s.src='https://algebra.fi/keranen/research/webJS/bookmarklet.js?'+Date.now();d.body.appendChild(s);})();



Name the bookmark something like "**Word Search**".



**Using the Bookmarklet**: First, navigate to the target page, then click the bookmarklet once the tab is open.



The opening panel has a draggable zoom handle in the lower right corner, so you can move and zoom the panel freely.
